Classic Margarita · Batch

The ratio is 3 Estrato : 1 orange liqueur : 2 lime : 1 agave syrup. Scale it up or down. Below is a three-bottle batch that fits a 1.5-gallon dispenser. Salt rim optional.
75 oz Estrato
3 × 750 ml bottles · 9⅜ cups
25 oz Orange liqueur
3 cups
50 oz Fresh lime juice
6¼ cups
25 oz Light agave syrup
3 cups

To make a drink: dispense 4 oz into a shaker filled with ice. Shake, then dirty dump into a 9 oz cup. Garnish with dehydrated lime.
