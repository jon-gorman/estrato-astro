deck: Tropical without turning into dessert.
ratio: "2 : 1 : 0.75 : 0.5"
glass: Rocks
rim: Salt optional
garnish: Pineapple leaf or lime wheel
method: Shaken
build:
  - amount: 2 oz
    item: Estrato
  - amount: 1 oz
    item: Pineapple juice
  - amount: 3/4 oz
    item: Fresh lime juice
  - amount: 1/2 oz
    item: Orange liqueur
steps:
  - Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  - Shake hard until cold and lightly frothy.
  - Strain over fresh ice and garnish with a pineapple leaf or lime wheel.
note: Add one thin jalapeño slice to the shaker when pineapple needs a little pushback.
