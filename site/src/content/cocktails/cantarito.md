name: Cantarito

blurb: Three kinds of citrus. One very good idea.
deck: Three kinds of citrus. One very good idea.
glass: Highball glass
rim: Tajín or salt
garnish: Lime, orange, and grapefruit wedges
method: Built
build:
  - amount: 2 oz
    item: Estrato
  - amount: 1 oz
    item: Fresh grapefruit juice
  - amount: 1/2 oz
    item: Fresh orange juice
  - amount: 1/2 oz
    item: Fresh lime juice
  - amount: 2 oz
    item: Grapefruit soda
  - amount: pinch
    item: Sea salt
steps:
  - Chill a tall highball glass in the freezer for a few minutes, or fill it with ice water while you prep.
  - Run a lime wedge around the rim and dip it in Tajín (a chili-lime seasoning) or coarse salt.
  - Fill the glass with fresh ice.
  - Add Estrato, grapefruit juice, orange juice, lime juice, and a small pinch of salt. Stir to chill.
  - Top with cold grapefruit soda and stir once, gently.
  - Garnish with lime, orange, and grapefruit wedges and serve with a straw.
note: A tall highball glass is all you need. Have a traditional clay cup? Soak it in cold water for 10 to 15 minutes first and use it the same way.
