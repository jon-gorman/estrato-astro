---
name: Spicy Margarita
order: 4
blurb: Bright citrus with a clean little kick.
deck: Bright citrus. Clean heat. No warning label required.
ratio: "2 : 0.75 : 0.75"
glass: Rocks
rim: Chile salt, half-rimmed
garnish: Lime wheel and jalapeño slice
method: Shaken
build:
  - amount: 2 oz
    item: Estrato
  - amount: 3/4 oz
    item: Orange liqueur
  - amount: 3/4 oz
    item: Fresh lime juice
  - amount: 2 slices
    item: Jalapeño
steps:
  - Add Estrato, orange liqueur, lime juice, and jalapeño to a shaker with ice.
  - Shake until cold and strain over fresh ice in a half-rimmed rocks glass.
  - Garnish with a lime wheel and one jalapeño slice.
note: For less heat, shake with one jalapeño slice. For more, add one extra slice — do not muddle unless you mean it.
