---
name: Agave Old Fashioned
order: 9
blurb: Short build. Long finish.
deck: Short build. Long finish.
glass: Rocks
garnish: Orange peel
method: Stirred
build:
  - amount: 2 oz
    item: Estrato
  - amount: 1/4 oz
    item: Agave syrup
  - amount: 2 dashes
    item: Orange bitters
steps:
  - Add Estrato, agave syrup, and bitters to a mixing glass with ice.
  - Stir until cold and strain over one large cube in a rocks glass.
  - Express an orange peel over the drink and use it as the garnish.
note: Start with less agave syrup and add more only after tasting.
